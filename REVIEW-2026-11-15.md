> **Revision 3 (same day, owner feedback).** Profile pictures replace the mood
> avatars, action tags no longer cover wagers, and "sats" wording became
> "bitcoin". See "Revision 3" at the end.
>
> **Revision 2 (same day, owner feedback).** See "Revision 2" at the end for

Scope: visual redesign of the table, phone layouts and account screens; a review
of gameplay, sockets, authentication and payment accounting with fixes and
regression tests. Nothing was deployed. No live node, relay publication, real
payment or production credential was used; payment tests use a fake node.

## Visual direction: arcade poker clubhouse

The wordmark's orange, sun yellow and ink outline now drive the whole interface.

- **Type.** Lilita One (the wordmark's face) for headings, buttons, card indices
  and tags; Nunito for names, amounts and body text. Both are self-hosted in
  `packages/frontend/fonts/` (OFL, licences in `scripts/licenses/`) with system
  fallbacks. No third-party font request is made.
- **Room and table.** Indigo room with a warm spotlight; a padded navy rail with
  orange piping and marquee bulbs; brighter teal felt. `table-felt.svg` and
  `table-rail.svg` keep the coordinate contract in `TABLE-ASSETS.md`, so the
  three room themes still work. The default theme is labelled "Neon clubhouse"
  (its id is still `bunker`).
- **Avatars.** A seat shows the player's Nostr profile picture. Players without
  one get a fixed "chip buddy" house avatar chosen from their public key
  (`js/avatars.js`). Phones now show avatars too. (The first version gave the
  house avatars changing expressions; revision 3 removed that.)
- **Turn indicator.** A ring around the acting avatar drains over the action
  time (green to red; cyan in the time bank), plus a glowing plate and a
  "Your move" / "Your turn" tag for the hero.
- **Readability.** Larger pot pill, wager pills, stacks and seat buttons. The
  last-action tag hangs off the plate edge instead of covering the name and
  stack. Your hole cards sit above your plate instead of on top of your avatar.
  A result banner under the board says who won, how much and with what.
- **Chips and cards.** Chips are redrawn with edge inserts and a denomination
  palette; card indices use the display face, suits are brighter, and the card
  back matches the brand.
- **Feedback.** Keycap buttons press down; a win shows the amount rising from
  the winner with a ten-spark burst (about 1.5 s, never looping). All of it is
  skipped under `prefers-reduced-motion`; state (winner tag, glow, turn ring)
  stays visible without movement.
- **Account screens.** The account dialog is left-aligned with a balance card, a
  Lightning status row with a status dot, clear disabled states and a separate
  custody note. Wording and payment logic are unchanged.

Before/after captures are kept with the review hand-off, not in the repository (`screenshots/` is git-ignored).

## Bugs fixed

Each item has a regression test unless noted.

### Money and accounts

1. **A failed withdrawal could be refunded twice.** The reconciler acted on rows
   read before it awaited the node; a withdrawal that failed and refunded during
   the pass was marked `failed` again and refunded a second time. Every
   reconcile decision now re-reads the row, and status changes are
   compare-and-set (`db.transitionLedgerStatus`).
2. **A deposit could be credited twice** when the invoice stream credited it
   while a stale "open and expired" lookup was in flight. Same fix.
3. **An account could be locked out of depositing** after one failed invoice
   request: the expired request was reused forever. It is now retired (still
   credits if ever reported paid).
4. **One unpaid invoice could reserve the whole deposit allowance.** New
   `MAX_DEPOSIT_SATS` (default 100,000) caps a single invoice.
5. **Receiving invoices about to expire were accepted**, which can leave a
   withdrawal the node never registered. They are now refused before anything is
   reserved.
6. **Withdrawal requests had no rate limit.** Now five per minute per player.
7. **Sign-in content bypassed profile limits** (a 12,000-character name or a
   `javascript:` picture were stored). The saved-profile limits now apply.

### Gameplay and connections

8. **All players shared one rate-limit bucket behind the proxy.** Sockets used
   the proxy's loopback address, and the action limit counted by address, so one
   player's rejected actions could make another's legal action fail and time
   out. Actions are limited per player; the client address is taken from the
   proxy's `X-Forwarded-For` entry only when the peer is loopback.
9. **After a crash recovery, players who reconnected and kept playing were
   folded and removed five minutes later** by the idle clock armed at restart.
   Reconnecting into a seat that is not sitting out now cancels it.
10. **A big blind all-in for no more than the small blind** still forced the
    small blind to "call 100 or fold"; a timeout folded a hand that was already
    covered.
11. **Standing up mid-hand toggled an earlier "sit out next hand" back off.**
12. **Disconnect grace was measured from an earlier disconnect.**
13. **A refused join (table full) stopped that socket's table updates.**
14. **Leaving mid-hand and re-joining returned a seat that was then removed**
    when the hand ended.
15. **A busted player could cancel the rebuy clock** with sit-back-in and hold
    the seat.
16. **The admin dashboard could not run** under the site's own Content Security
    Policy (inline script and handlers). The script is now `/admin.js`; its HTML
    escaper also escapes quotes. Checked in a browser, no automated test.

### Interface

17. **A typed or dragged raise was reset to the minimum** whenever any table
    update arrived during your turn.
18. **Broken profile pictures never fell back** (the inline `onerror` is blocked
    by the CSP), and avatars were rebuilt on every update.
19. **Open-seat buttons were rebuilt on every update**, dropping keyboard focus.
20. **Chip colours and labels from the server were written into SVG markup.**
    Artwork is now chosen on the client by denomination.
21. **Layout:** hero cards covered the hero avatar; the action tag covered name
    and stack and clipped on phones ("RAISE TO 300"); side-seat wagers slid
    under plates; phone wagers overlapped the pot and each other.

## Remaining issues

- **Circuit breaker is sticky.** One transient node read error trips it, and only
  a restart clears it (`resetBreaker` has no caller). Left unchanged because the
  pause-until-reviewed posture is an operator decision; consider not tripping on
  read errors or adding an admin reset.
- **Solvency check may double-count in-flight withdrawals** if the node's channel
  balance already excludes the pending HTLC. Unverified against a real node.
- **A withdrawal the node rejected before registering stays `in_flight`** and is
  re-sent each pass. Fix 5 removes the known trigger; the code still never
  refunds from "not found", by design. An expired, never-registered payment
  needs an operator procedure or an explicit rule.
- **Operator-paid routing fee can exceed a tiny withdrawal** (51-sat budget on a
  1-sat withdrawal; an existing test asserts this policy). The new rate limit
  bounds the rate, not the ratio. Consider a minimum withdrawal or a fee cap
  relative to the amount.
- **Deposit allowance can still be exhausted by many identities**, each within
  the per-invoice cap. Needs a per-address pending budget.
- **`nostr-tools` loads from esm.sh at runtime without integrity pinning**, and it
  handles private keys in the private-key sign-in path. Self-host or pin it.
- Recovery can log a duplicate `*** SHOW DOWN ***` line (cosmetic).
- Small desktop windows scale the whole table, so table text gets small near
  1180 × 720. On phones an action tag can briefly overlap a neighbour's cards.
- The release checks in `TODO.md` still apply. This review does not establish
  that the app is free of bugs or ready for real money.

## Revision 2 — owner feedback

1. **Action colours follow a stop light.** Fold is red, check/call is neutral
   grey, bet/raise is green. The last-action tags on nameplates use the same
   colours.
2. **No top bar.** On desktop its contents are two small corner clusters: table
   identity (top left) and `Account · ₿ N` plus `Menu` (top right). Name, Edit
   profile and Sign out moved into the Table options menu, as on phones. The
   table now starts 8px from the top; at 1440 × 900 its scale went from 0.76 to
   0.89 (about 17% larger). On phones the bar is gone entirely; seat count,
   blinds and buy-in range are in the Table options summary, and the felt
   carries the wordmark.
3. **Amounts read `₿ 5,000`, without the word "sats".** Applied wherever the
   symbol and the word appeared together (account button, account dialog,
   buy-in, deposits, username purchase). Labels such as "Deposit sats" and
   "Amount in sats" are unchanged. Five test assertions were updated to match.
4. **Phone wagers belong to their cards.** In portrait each wager sits directly
   beneath the cards of top-row seats and directly above the cards of
   bottom-row seats, including your own; nothing floats mid-table. In landscape
   the felt is too short for that, so each wager sits beside its owner's cards
   on the inner side.
5. **Landscape side seats hug the rail** (9% / 91% instead of 15% / 85%).
6. **Mucking is enforced by the server.** Previously every live hand was
   revealed to everyone at showdown and written to the public hand history.
   Now:
   - If any player still in the hand is all-in, all live hands are tabled.
   - Otherwise the last player to bet or raise on the river shows first (with no
     river bet, the first live player left of the button), then clockwise; a
     hand that cannot beat or tie the best hand already shown is mucked.
   - A mucked hand is never sent to other players or spectators, never written
     to the shared hand log, stored hand history, other players' histories or
     the published history. Its owner still sees their own cards.
   - Operator records (`hand_players`, the admin-only event log) still hold
     every dealt card for dispute resolution.
   - The "Auto-Muck Losers" toggle was removed: it never did anything.
   Six engine tests and one rendering test cover this. Not offered: a way for a
   player to voluntarily show a losing or uncalled hand.

Checked the same way as before (headless Chrome, 1440 × 900, 390 × 844,
844 × 390, plus 375 × 667). Still not checked on a physical phone.

## Revision 3 — owner feedback

1. **Nostr profile pictures are the avatar.** A player with a profile picture is
   shown by that picture alone. The house avatar appears only when a player has
   no picture or the picture fails to load, and it is now one fixed face per
   player: the changing expressions were removed. Folded, all-in, winner and
   turn states are still shown by dimming, tags, the glow and the turn ring.
   Pictures already reached the table (fetched from relays at sign-in and kept
   in the player record); nothing changed on the server for this.
2. **Action tags never cover a wager.** On phones the last action ("Call 300")
   now takes the name line inside the nameplate for a moment instead of hanging
   off its edge, so it cannot sit on a wager, cards or a neighbouring seat. The
   stack stays visible beneath it. "Raise to 1,200" is shortened to
   "Raise 1,200" to fit. Desktop tags still hang below the plate, where nothing
   else is placed.
3. **Wording.** "Deposit sats", "Withdraw sats", "Amount in sats", "Add sats to
   account", "Real sats" and similar now say "bitcoin". The two amount entry
   fields read "Amount in bitcoin (sats)" so the unit of the number typed is
   unambiguous. One test assertion was
   updated to match. Server-side error text that states a plain number of sats
   (for example a maximum withdrawal) was left as is.

Checked with five scripted opponents, three with stand-in profile pictures
served locally, one with a deliberately broken picture URL (falls back to the
house avatar) and one with none. Real Nostr profiles from live relays were not
exercised. 331 tests pass.
